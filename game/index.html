SUNSET HARBOR

Lucia Vega is out of lockup and the city is hers for the taking. Steal cars, dodge the Harbor Patrol, run six story missions and pull off the Sunset Score — all before the sun goes down.

Keyboard + mouse · autosaves as you play · works offline

How to Play

W A S D / Arrows — walk · Shift — sprint
Mouse — aim · Click / F — shoot the pistol
E — steal / enter / exit a car
W / S — drive · A / D — steer
Esc / P — pause

Crimes raise your wanted level. Lay low and the heat dies down. Get busted and you pay a fine; get wasted and you wake up in the clinic. Follow the yellow marker to run the story.
